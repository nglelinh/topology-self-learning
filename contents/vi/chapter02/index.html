💡 Tóm tắt Chapter

Chapter 02 introduced two fundamental topological properties:

  • Connectedness: Space không thể chia thành disjoint open pieces. IVT = application!
  • Compactness: Every open cover has finite subcover. "Finite-like" behavior.
  • Heine-Borel: Trong ℝⁿ, compact ⇔ closed & bounded (beautiful characterization!)
  • Local compactness: Each point has compact neighborhood. ℝⁿ locally compact.
  • Compactification: Add "point at infinity" to complete non-compact spaces.

Hai properties này appear everywhere trong topology, analysis, geometry, và physics. Tiếp theo: separation axioms—distinguishing points and sets!
