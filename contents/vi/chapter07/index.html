Chapter 07: Homology Theory

Homology theory là một trong những công cụ powerful nhất trong algebraic topology. While fundamental group π₁ detects "1-dimensional holes" (loops), homology groups H_n detect holes of all dimensions. Homology is computable, functorial, và provides complete algebraic invariants cho nhiều spaces. Chapter này introduces simplicial và singular homology, computational techniques (Mayer-Vietoris), và cohomology với cup product structure.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand chain complexes và boundary operators
  • Define simplicial và singular homology
  • Compute H_n(X) cho standard spaces
  • Apply Mayer-Vietoris sequence
  • Understand Euler characteristic via homology
  • Work with cohomology và cup product
  • Connect homology với topology
  • Apply homology to classification problems

🎓 Chapter Summary

Chapter 07 introduced homology và cohomology—powerful algebraic invariants:

  • Chain Complex: C_n → C_(n-1) với ∂∘∂ = 0
  • Homology: H_n = ker(∂_n) / im(∂_(n+1))
  • Simplicial: From triangulations
  • Singular: Works for any space, homotopy invariant
  • Computations: H_*(Sⁿ) = ℤ (k=0,n), 0 (else)
  • Mayer-Vietoris: Compute from pieces
  • Cohomology: Dual với additional structure (cup product)

Key Results

  • Homotopy Invariance: X ≃ Y ⇒ H_*(X) ≅ H_*(Y)
  • Euler Characteristic: χ = Σ(-1)ⁿ rank(H_n)
  • Mayer-Vietoris: Long exact sequence for unions
  • Universal Coefficient: Relates H^n với H_n
  • Poincaré Duality: H^k(M) ≅ H_(n-k)(M) for manifolds

Applications

  • Classification: Distinguish spaces via homology groups
  • Fixed Points: Lefschetz theorem
  • Vector Fields: Hairy ball theorem
  • Data Analysis: Persistent homology (TDA)
