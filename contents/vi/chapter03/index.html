🔍 Chapter 03: Separation Axioms and Metrization

Tiên đề Phân ly và Metric hóa

Không phải mọi topological space đều "nice"! Separation axioms (T₀, T₁, T₂, T₃, T₄) classify spaces theo khả năng distinguish points và sets. Metrization theorems tell us when topology có thể define bởi metric.

🎯 Mục tiêu Học tập

  • Hiểu hierarchy của separation axioms (T₀ → T₁ → T₂ → T₃ → T₄)
  • Master Hausdorff (T₂) spaces—most important!
  • Thành thạo Regular và Normal spaces, Urysohn's Lemma
  • Understand countability axioms (first, second countable)
  • Nắm vững Urysohn's Metrization Theorem

💡 Tóm tắt Chapter

Chapter 03 classified topological spaces by "niceness":

  • T₀ → T₁ → T₂ → T₃ → T₄: Hierarchy of separation (stronger = nicer)
  • Hausdorff (T₂): Most important—unique limits, closed diagonal
  • Regular (T₃), Normal (T₄): Separate closed sets via open sets
  • Urysohn's Lemma: Separate closed sets via continuous functions!
  • Countability: First countable (sequences), second countable (separable)
  • Metrization: Regular + 2nd countable ⇒ metric exists (Urysohn)

These axioms tell us when spaces behave nicely (limits unique, extension theorems work, metric exists). Tiếp theo: metric spaces in depth!
