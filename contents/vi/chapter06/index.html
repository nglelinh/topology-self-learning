Chapter 06: Fundamental Group and Covering Spaces

Chapter này marks the beginning của algebraic topology—using algebraic structures (groups) để study topological spaces. Fundamental group π₁(X, x₀) assigns một group cho mỗi pointed space, capturing thông tin về loops và "holes". Covering space theory provides powerful machinery cho computing π₁ và understanding global topology from local structure. Van Kampen's theorem enables computation of π₁ for complex spaces from simpler pieces.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand homotopy và path homotopy
  • Define và compute fundamental group π₁(X, x₀)
  • Master covering space theory
  • Apply lifting properties
  • Compute π₁ using Van Kampen's theorem
  • Understand deck transformations
  • Work with universal covers
  • Connect topology với group theory

🎓 Chapter Summary

Chapter 06 introduced fundamental group và covering spaces—foundation của algebraic topology:

  • Homotopy: Continuous deformation, homotopy equivalence
  • π₁(X, x₀): Group of homotopy classes of loops at basepoint
  • Examples: π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ, π₁(Sⁿ) = 0 (n≥2), π₁(T²) ≅ ℤ²
  • Covering Spaces: p: X̃ → X locally homeomorphic
  • Universal Cover: X̃ simply connected, X = X̃/π₁(X)
  • Lifting Properties: Paths và homotopies lift uniquely
  • Van Kampen: Compute π₁(X) from π₁(A), π₁(B), π₁(A∩B)

Key Theorems

  • π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ: Winding number
  • Path Lifting: Unique lifts of paths in covering spaces
  • Covering Classification: X̃/Aut(X̃/X) ≅ X
  • Van Kampen: π₁(A ∪ B) ≅ π₁(A) *_{π₁(A∩B)} π₁(B)

Applications

  • Topology: Distinguish spaces (S¹ ≠ S²)
  • Fixed Points: Brouwer theorem via π₁
  • Complex Analysis: Riemann surfaces, monodromy
  • Physics: Gauge theory, magnetic monopoles
