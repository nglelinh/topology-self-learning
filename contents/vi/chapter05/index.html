Chapter 05: Quotient Spaces and Surfaces

Quotient topology là một trong những constructions powerful nhất trong topology—cách "glue things together" hay "identify points". Chapter này explores quotient spaces, classification of compact surfaces (một trong những achievements đẹp nhất của topology), CW complexes (framework để build spaces from cells), và group actions (formalizing symmetry). Những công cụ này essential cho algebraic topology và differential geometry.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Master quotient topology và universal property
  • Understand surface classification theorem
  • Compute Euler characteristic và genus
  • Build spaces using CW complexes
  • Work với group actions và orbit spaces
  • Apply quotient constructions (S¹, T², ℝP^n)
  • Understand orientability và topological invariants
  • Connect với covering space theory

🎓 Chapter Summary

Chapter 05 introduced quotient topology và applications—powerful tools để construct và classify spaces:

  • Quotient topology: U open ⟺ π⁻¹(U) open—finest topology making projection continuous
  • Universal property: f: X/~ → Y continuous ⟺ f ∘ π continuous
  • Examples: S¹ = [0,1]/(0~1), T² = square với edges glued, ℝP^n = S^n với antipodal identified
  • Surface classification: All compact connected surfaces = {S², Σ_g, ℝP²^#k}
  • Invariants: Euler characteristic χ, orientability, genus g
  • Formula: χ = 2 - 2g (orientable), χ = 2 - k (non-orientable)
  • CW complexes: Build spaces by attaching cells D^n inductively
  • Cellular homology: Compute H_n directly from cell structure
  • Group actions: G × X → X, orbit space X/G
  • Free + properly discontinuous: Nice quotients, covering spaces

Key Theorems

  • Universal Property: Quotient topology characterized by maps from quotient
  • Surface Classification: Complete classification by χ và orientability
  • Euler Formula: χ = V - E + F (triangulation), χ = 2 - 2g (genus g)
  • Gauss-Bonnet: ∫∫K dA = 2πχ (curvature ↔ topology)
  • Whitehead Theorem: CW approximation, homotopy equivalence
  • Orbit-Stabilizer: |G · x| · |G_x| = |G|

Applications

  • Algebraic Topology: CW complexes framework, cellular homology
  • Differential Geometry: Surfaces, Gauss-Bonnet, homogeneous spaces
  • Covering Spaces: X̃/π₁(X) = X (universal cover quotient)
  • Lie Groups: Homogeneous spaces G/H (spheres, projective spaces)
  • Physics: Gauge theory (principal bundles), symmetry
  • Crystallography: Space groups, wallpaper groups
